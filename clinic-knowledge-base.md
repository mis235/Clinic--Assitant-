## Treatment details

### Acne scar treatment
Category: Acne Scar
Also called / patients may say: acne scar, acne scars, scars, scar, pits, ice pick, boxcar, rolling scar, acne marks, pimple marks, daag, muhase ke daag
- What it is: Acne scars are treated with three main technologies, chosen by scar type: Dermapen microneedling for mild scars and texture, MNRF for deeper ice-pick and boxcar scars, and CO2 laser for severe acne damage. RF subcision is used for tethered rolling scars.
- Who it's for: Ice-pick, rolling and boxcar acne scars, open pores, uneven texture, and stretch marks or other scars on the face and body.
- How it's done: The doctor examines your scars and builds a personalised plan, often combining two or more treatments. Settings are chosen to be safe for Indian skin.
- Time per session: Depends on the treatment chosen; most sessions take 45 to 90 minutes including numbing cream.
- Sessions: Usually a series of sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart. The doctor sets the number after assessment.
- Comfort / pain: Numbing cream is applied first, so most people feel only mild pricking or warmth.
- Downtime: Dermapen: 12 to 48 hours of mild redness. MNRF: 2 to 3 days of redness and swelling. CO2 laser: 5 to 10 days of social downtime.
- Before the visit: Stop retinoids and acids 3 to 5 days before, avoid sun tanning, and tell the doctor about all your medicines.
- Aftercare: Use sunscreen strictly, a gentle cleanser and moisturiser, don't pick the skin, and avoid active skincare until it heals.
- Results: Scars improve gradually as new collagen forms over 3 to 6 months. The aim is a clear, natural improvement rather than complete removal.
- Side effects: Temporary redness, swelling and a small chance of darkening, which is why settings are adjusted for Indian skin.

### CO2 laser resurfacing
Category: Acne Scar
Also called / patients may say: co2, co2 laser, fractional co2, resurfacing, laser resurfacing
- What it is: A fractional CO2 laser removes microscopic columns of damaged skin and triggers new collagen, improving scars, wrinkles, texture and firmness.
- Who it's for: Moderate to severe acne scars, wrinkles and fine lines, surgical or injury scars, sun damage and age spots, rough texture, large pores, mild skin laxity and stretch marks.
- How it's done: After a detailed consultation, numbing cream is applied for 45 to 60 minutes and eye protection is given. The laser is delivered precisely, and you may feel warmth or prickling. Soothing ointment and sunscreen are applied at the end.
- Time per session: About 1.5 to 2 hours including numbing.
- Sessions: Often one session. Deeper scars may need 2 to 3.
- Comfort / pain: Numbing cream is used. Most people feel heat or mild prickling.
- Downtime: 5 to 10 days of redness and swelling.
- Before the visit: Stop retinoids and exfoliating products 3 to 5 days before, avoid sun tanning, and tell the doctor if you get cold sores.
- Aftercare: Use the soothing ointment, cleanse and moisturise gently, and protect from sun strictly. Don't pick the crusts; let them fall off naturally.
- Results: The skin heals in 1 to 2 weeks. Collagen keeps building and the final result shows in 3 to 6 months.
- Side effects: Redness, swelling and a risk of temporary darkening in Indian skin, which the clinic minimises with calibrated settings and strict aftercare.

### RF subcision
Category: Acne Scar
Also called / patients may say: subcision, rf subcision, tethered scar

- Who it's for: Rolling, tethered, boxcar and ice-pick acne scars.

- Sessions: Usually 1 to 2 sessions, as advised by the doctor.
